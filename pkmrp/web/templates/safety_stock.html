Safety stock

The question this page answers: which safety stock numbers in MAX shouldn't we plan to? PK-MRP keeps MAX's safety stock on bought parts as a floor: after every booked order is covered, stock below it is a buy, ranked after the buys that protect an order. But a safety stock is set by hand and rarely revisited, so each one is judged first. Ignored: the part is on hold, replaced, or obsolete at its maker, or nobody used it in the last 12 months and nothing is booked. Capped: it's more than 6 months of real use (the larger of the last 12 months' issues and shipments, and the booked rate), so PK-MRP holds 6 months. The rest are trusted and planned as set.

Fix the number in MAX (Part Master, Safety Stock) or leave it if it's deliberate — a part stays here until MAX and use agree. Download every part with safety stock (CSV).
