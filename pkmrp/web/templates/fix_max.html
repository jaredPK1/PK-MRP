Fix in MAX

The question this page answers: what in MAX still names a part Engineering obsoleted? When an ECN obsoletes a part, the planning bills get switched, but orders, forecast entries and BOMs entered earlier keep the old part — and MRP keeps buying it. This list is rebuilt every plan from Ron's ECN app and MAX; a row disappears by itself once MAX is corrected.
