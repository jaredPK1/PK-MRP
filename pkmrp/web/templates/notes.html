Part notes

The question this page answers: what do we know about a part that MAX doesn't say? Engineering working on a replacement, the part going obsolete, an ECN on the way, a supplier problem. A note is said once and stays on the part — it shows as a chip next to the part number on every page, every plan — until someone removes it. Notes don't change the plan, except Hold — don't buy yet: a held part comes off every buy list until the hold is removed.

Replacements — plan the new part where the old one is still called for

When Engineering replaces a part but open orders, work orders or BOMs in MAX still name the old one, PK-MRP would keep buying the old part. A replacement makes the plan use the new part instead — order demand, WO requirements and BOMs all move. Tick use up if the old part's stock and open POs can be used for the same thing (backward compatible); otherwise they show on Overbuys. Takes effect on the next hourly plan. The orders in MAX still need fixing — this keeps buying right until they are.
