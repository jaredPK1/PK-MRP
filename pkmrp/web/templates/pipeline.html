Pipeline — which MAX part is each sales SKU?

The question this page answers: what does Book/Bill 2.0's forecast mean in MAX parts? Sales books "2300AG-B" or "FK11-SAE-U"; MAX knows CF-0016-00G and 01-0351-00G. Until a SKU is matched to a part, its forecast can't reach the committed parts it would need. PK-MRP proposes a match from two kinds of evidence — the name (the part number or MAX description says it) and past orders (on bookings that became SOs, what MAX actually put on the order) — and a person confirms it. Nothing is used until it's confirmed.
