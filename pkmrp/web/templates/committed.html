Committed parts — planned a year ahead

The question this page answers: which parts can't wait for an order? Most parts are bought only when a customer order needs them. A few take longer to arrive than orders give warning — their real lead time, measured from their own past POs, is more than about 63 working days (~13 weeks). Those are planned 12 months out: a requirement, a buffer, a proposed blanket order with monthly releases, and a forecast for the supplier.
