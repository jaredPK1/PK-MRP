The Monday email

PK-MRP's one email. Goes out Mondays at 7 am Pacific through pk-notify. On top is Needs you this week, most urgent kind first: buys past their order-by, parts going obsolete with nothing to cover them, Done that MAX doesn't show, recent overbuys, price issues, repeat POs. Up to five lines each, NEW marked (not in last Monday's email), and every part links to the page that explains it. Last week's numbers come after. Nothing else in PK-MRP sends email.
