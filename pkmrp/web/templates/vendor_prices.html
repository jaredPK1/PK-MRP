Biggest dollars first. Price up: the latest PO costs 10%+ more per unit than the one before, from the same vendor, in the last 4 months or still open. Over standard: an open PO 20%+ over standard cost. Cheaper elsewhere: another vendor sold us the part 15%+ cheaper in the last two years. Vendor-wide: the same parts cost 8%+ more than a year ago on $25K+ of spend. Check the unit: a price 4x off the last one — usually a pack/each slip. Each needs $2,000+ at stake (a unit slip shows from $200).
