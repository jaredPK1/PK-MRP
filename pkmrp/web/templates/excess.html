Excess stock

The question this page answers: what stock does the plan not need, and how stale is it? It's the starting list for the excess & obsolete reserve. Every part PK makes or buys is measured against what PK-MRP plans for it: everyday parts against every booked order and open work order (a subassembly only counts the demand its parents pass down after their own stock) plus the safety stock PK-MRP holds; long-lead parts, bought a year ahead on purpose, against their 12-month requirement plus buffer. MAX's -FAMILY forecast entries don't count. Valued per each at MAX cost (cost ÷ the cost-unit conversion, so reels and packs aren't 1,000× high).

Bulk (floor stock) parts aren't planned, so they aren't here. Download every part (CSV) — the tiers are for Finance to put percentages on.
