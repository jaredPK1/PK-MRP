EOL watch

The question this page answers: which parts are makers ending, and what should we buy before they go? From three places: DigiKey / Mouser / Octopart lifecycle status, PCN and end-of-life notices in the purchasing mailbox (read by Claude), and a weekly web search of the makers behind our most-booked parts. Each part number is matched to ours through MAX's manufacturer part numbers. Last-time buy = booked orders plus two years at the yearly rate, less stock and open POs — a starting point for the conversation with Engineering, not an order.
