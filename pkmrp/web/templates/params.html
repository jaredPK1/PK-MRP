Lead times — fix the numbers in MAX

The question this page answers: which lead times stored in MAX are wrong? Nothing here is a buy. It compares the lead time in MAX (PURLT) with how long each part has actually taken on its own past POs, and lists the ones more than 25% off, with a proposed number — the slow end of that history (or the median, with fewer than 5 receipts), rounded up to 5 working days. Someone keys the ones they agree with into MAX; PK-MRP never writes there.

Why bother: MAX's own MRP — and the order-by dates everyone else sees — run on these numbers. Too long and things get ordered early (overbuys); too short and they arrive late. Which parts get planned a year ahead is a different question — that's Committed.
